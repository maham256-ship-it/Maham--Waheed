Integrative In Silico Analysis of Overexpressed Genes in TNBC and the EZH2 Network

Independent project, Nov 2026 · full manuscript prepared

A five-layer multi-omics workflow integrating transcript, protein, promoter-methylation, mutation and protein-interaction data.

Identified the 25 most overexpressed genes in triple-negative breast cancer against normal breast tissue, dominated by mitotic regulators (UBE2C, FOXM1, CDC20, AURKB, PLK1) and extracellular-matrix genes (MMP1, MMP11, COL11A1, COL10A1)
Showed higher TK1 and P4HA1 protein in primary tumours (n = 125) than normal tissue (n = 18) using CPTAC proteomics
Found lower S100P promoter methylation in tumours — median β ≈ 0.45 against 0.65 (n = 793 and 97)
Profiled the CPTAC breast cancer mutational landscape (126 samples; TP53 38.9%, PIK3CA 31.0%) and mapped the EZH2 network linking PRC2 partners SUZ12, EED, RBBP4 and JARID2 to DNMT1, DNMT3B and HDAC1
Wrote the study up in full — abstract, methods, five figures, discussion and limitations — keeping the interpretation conservative and correlative, and nominated TK1, P4HA1 and S100P for experimental validation

Tools: UALCAN (TCGA BRCA, CPTAC) · cBioPortal · STRING
